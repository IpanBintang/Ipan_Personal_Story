My Hobbies & Interests

Outside of my studies, I make it a point to engage in activities that recharge my energy and bring me joy. Hobbies are not just pastimes — they are a window into a person's character, creativity, and values. Here are the things I love to do most in my free time.

📷 Photography

Photography is my favourite hobby. I started taking photos when I was in secondary school using just my phone, and I have grown to appreciate the art of capturing moments, light, and emotion. I particularly enjoy street photography and nature photography.

I am currently saving up for a proper DSLR camera. For now, I use my smartphone and experiment with different editing apps. You can see some of my work on the Gallery page.

📚 Reading

I have loved reading since I was a child. Books open up worlds that you cannot experience any other way. My favourite genres include self-improvement, science fiction, and biographies.

Some books that have truly impacted my life:

  • Atomic Habits by James Clear
  • The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho
  • Sapiens by Yuval Noah Harari

Learn more about my favourite authors at Goodreads.

🎮 Video Games

Video games are one of my greatest passions. I love how games blend storytelling, art, and challenge into a single interactive experience. Whether it is a fast-paced action platformer or a deep role-playing adventure, I am always up for a good session.

My top 3 favourite games right now:

  • Sonic x Shadow Generations — by SEGA
  • Hollow Knight — by Team Cherry
  • Persona 5 Royal — by ATLUS

Each of these games has left a lasting impression on me — from the blazing speed of Sonic, to the hauntingly beautiful world of Hollow Knight, to the stylish storytelling of Persona 5 Royal.

🎵 Music

Music is something I turn to every single day — whether I am studying, relaxing, or on the go. The right song can completely change your mood and mindset. I have a wide taste in music, but a few genres speak to me more than others.

My favourite music genres:

  • Synthwave — that nostalgic 80s/90s retro vibe with electronic beats
  • Violin Instrumental — calm, emotional, and deeply moving
  • J-Pop — energetic and refreshing Japanese pop music

“Music gives a soul to the universe, wings to the mind, and life to everything.” — Plato. I could not agree more.
