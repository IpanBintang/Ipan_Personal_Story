Photography

Photography is my favourite hobby. I started taking photos in secondary school using just my phone, and I've grown to appreciate the art of capturing moments, light, and emotion.

I particularly enjoy street photography and nature photography. I'm currently saving up for a DSLR. For now, I experiment with different editing apps and my smartphone.

You can see some of my work on the Gallery page.

Reading

I've loved reading since I was a child. Books open up worlds you cannot experience any other way. My favourite genres include self-improvement, science fiction, and biographies.

Books that have truly impacted me:

  • Atomic Habits — James Clear

03

Badminton

Badminton is my go-to sport. I play at least twice a week with friends and housemates at the campus sports hall. Great for staying active while having fun and building friendships.

I've been playing since I was 10 — my father taught me the basics and it became a family tradition ever since. I especially admire Lee Chong Wei — a true legend of Malaysian badminton.

Since 2015 2x / week Intermediate

04

Cooking

Cooking is something I discovered during the pandemic and I haven't stopped since. There's something deeply satisfying about creating a meal from scratch and sharing it with people you care about.

I enjoy cooking Malaysian traditional dishes — nasi goreng, rendang, and mee goreng are my specialties. I often cook for my family during weekends.

"Cooking is love made visible."

— A motto I truly believe
